The Diplomat, with Rena Bitter

Every day, all around the world, 13,000 U.S. Foreign Service Officers are responsible for a potentially life-changing decision: Should the person in front of me get a visa to the United States? Rena Bitter oversaw this global operation as Assistant Secretary of State for Consular Affairs, the capstone of her 30-year career as a diplomat and ambassador. Now retired, she can speak freely about how the visa process really works, and how Trump 2.0 is dramatically changing who gets to travel and study here, along with the nature of the Foreign Service itself. She also tells us what it took to carry out the largest airlift in American history to rescue our Afghan allies, why she loves international students, and how in government you have to know when the juice is worth the squeeze.
