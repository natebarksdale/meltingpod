Biden and the Border, Part 1

In this episode of The Melting Pod, co-hosts Doug Rand, Claire Trickler-McNulty, and Amanda Baran begin a two-part deep dive into the reality of the border during the Biden administration. They are joined by Charanya Krishnaswami, who served as a senior advisor to the Secretary of Homeland Security for all four years of the administration. Together, they pull back the curtain on why seemingly competent people struggled to “fix” an immigration system that became a humanitarian, operational, and political crisis.

The conversation moves past simplistic political narratives to explore the “morass” of policies inherited in 2021. The team discusses the decision to maintain Title 42, the “dark magic trick” of using public health law to bypass asylum processes, and how that choice led to inflated border numbers and a perception of chaos.

From the internal reaction to the imagery of the Del Rio crisis to the “tortured” implementation of the Asylum Officer Rule, this episode provides a candid post-mortem of the administration’s first two years. It is an essential look at the friction between high-level policy goals and the reality of moving a massive federal battleship.
