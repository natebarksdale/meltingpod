Welcome to The Melting Pod

The Melting Pod brings you stories of our dysfunctional immigration system, and where we go from here.

In this inaugural episode, Claire reveals how everything going on with ICE is even worse than it looks; Amanda explains how Trump is *de*-legalizing millions of people who thought they were protected; and Doug shatters any remaining illusions that MAGA is okay with legal immigrants.

Plus we introduce ourselves, drag the New York Times a bit, and give you a few things to be hopeful about in 2026. Join us!

In This Episode

  1. Intro
  2. Was anyone prepared for this?
  3. Minneapolis is different from anything that came before
  4. The DHS funding fight – high stakes, weak leverage
  5. If Claire were in charge, what would Democrats prioritize?
  6. How the ICE surge is taking real cops off the beat
  7. Deaths in detention
  8. How Trump is de-legalizing immigrants with permission to be here
  9. How Trump is choking off legal immigration
  10. Litigation heroes fighting the immigration ban
  11. Birthright citizenship – what happens next?
  12. Claire’s story: “How can we shut her up”
  13. Amanda’s story: “I hated law school with a passion”
  14. Doug’s story: “I took some detours”
  15. Memo to NYTimes: Trump is just lying to you
  16. Things to actually hope for in 2026?
  17. Closing
