Biden and the Border, Part 2

In the second half of our deep dive into the Biden administration’s border policy, we pick up in 2023 at the end of Title 42. Former DHS counselor Charanya Krishnaswami joins Claire Trickler-McNulty, Amanda Baran, and Doug Rand to dismantle the simplistic narratives that dominate the news cycle. We move past the punditry to examine the “carrot and stick” approach of the CBP One app, the political theater of migrant busing, and the internal friction over “pull factors.”

Krishnaswami offers a candid look at the administration’s struggle to balance a humanitarian vision against an obsession with border numbers. We discuss why the much-hyped 2024 bipartisan border bill was viewed by many insiders as a messaging exercise that traded away asylum protections for nothing in return. Finally, we move toward a constructive path forward, outlining a tactical five-point plan for any future administration looking to build a system based on regional diplomacy, clear legal standards, and operational reality rather than fear.

This isn’t a story of heroes and villains—it’s an unvarnished look at why acting in good faith is often insufficient within a rigid, outdated bureaucracy.
